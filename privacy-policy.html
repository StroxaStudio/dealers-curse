Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 13, 2026

This policy explains the types of information that may be processed when you play Dealer’s Curse, why they are used, and how to contact us about your data.

1. Who We Are

Dealer’s Curse is a mobile game developed and published by Stroxa Studio. For privacy questions, contact stroxastudio@gmail.com.

2. Information Used by the Game

Depending on the features you use, the game may process the following categories of information:

  • Account information: when you choose Google Sign-In, an account identifier and basic profile information made available through the sign-in process may be used to associate your game account.
  • Gameplay information: saved progress, stages completed, in-game achievements, and leaderboard-related data may be associated with your account.
  • Technical information: limited technical and diagnostic information may be processed by service providers to operate, secure, and troubleshoot the game, depending on the services enabled in the released version.

3. Why We Use Information

Information is used to authenticate players, save and restore progress, provide leaderboard features, maintain game functionality, address support requests, and protect the service.

4. Services and Data Sharing

Dealer’s Curse uses Google services, including Google Sign-In and Firebase, for account and game-related functionality. These providers may process information on our behalf according to their applicable terms and privacy policies. We do not claim that personal data is never shared: information required to operate these services may be transmitted to them.

5. Data Retention

Account-associated gameplay data may be retained while your game account remains active. If you request account deletion, we will review and process deletion of associated account and game data, subject to any information that must be retained for legitimate security, legal, or operational reasons. See our Account Deletion page for instructions.

6. Your Choices

You may choose whether to use Google Sign-In where the game provides that choice. You can contact us to request deletion of your game account and associated personal data. Removing the app from your device does not necessarily delete cloud-stored account information.

7. Children’s Privacy

We do not intentionally collect personal information from children in violation of applicable law. Parents or guardians who have concerns can contact us at the email address below.

8. Security

We use third-party platform services and reasonable measures intended to protect game information. No electronic storage or transmission method is completely secure.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when game features or data practices change. The updated date at the top of this page will reflect revisions.

10. Contact

For privacy questions or data requests, email stroxastudio@gmail.com.
